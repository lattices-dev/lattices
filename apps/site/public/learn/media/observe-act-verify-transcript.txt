A click is not a completed task

Opening: Lattices / Field Guide. RELIABLE UI AUTOMATION. A click is not a completed task. Observe the app. Act once. Check what changed.

[0–10 seconds] Observe. Goal: save Weekend plans. Find the right window and the Save control.

[10–20 seconds] Act. Press Save once. An accepted action does not prove the file was saved.

[20–30 seconds] Verify. The app reports “Could not save”. The error means the goal is still unmet.

[30–40 seconds] Recover with a limit. Inspect the cause before retrying. If one safe correction fails, stop and ask for help.

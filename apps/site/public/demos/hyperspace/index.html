Tideline: Phase-Aligned Scheduling for Long-Horizon Agents
PDFOther formats

Authors: R. Okafor, M. Lindqvist, S. Tan, et al.

[v1] 4 Nov 2026 · arXiv:2611.04127

Abstract

We present Tideline, a scheduler that aligns agent subtasks to shared phases of a planning horizon. Instead of interleaving work greedily, Tideline defers each task to the next slack phase, which keeps long-horizon plans consistent while reducing redundant tool calls. Across three planning suites it improves task completion at equal compute.

Lattices is a programmable workspace for you and your agents. You arrange the
windows, choose the projects, and decide which computers belong in your working
environment. An agent can use those same workspace controls when you give it
access.

That is the direction behind the recent work, and the reason for the new line
on the homepage: **Your workspace, programmable.**

## Start with your own workspace

The useful unit is the work you are doing: the terminal for a project, the
windows around it, and the layout that lets you pick it up again. Lattices
connects those pieces through shortcuts, workspace layers, the command bar,
and an API.

A script can find a window, place it, and inspect the resulting layout. An
agent can use those operations too. The workspace stays recognizable whichever
way you control it.

## Connect the computers you choose

The same idea extends to a connected computer. Remote access is an explicit
choice: you start the gateway, pair a client, and approve the operations it
can use. Access is scoped to specific products and methods.

Each host reports its capabilities. Mac and Linux support different operations,
so a connected computer offers the controls it actually has. The
[remote-host documentation](/lattices/docs/api#remote-hosts) covers setup and
permissions.

## A family of focused tools

Lattices manages the workspace. [Action](/action) handles computer-use tasks.
[Canvas](/canvas) keeps spatial notes, and [Voice](/voice) provides speech
playback. They are independent apps with their own lifecycles, connected where
that helps your work.

We are preparing the latest workspace and remote-viewing improvements for the
next release. This update describes the product direction; the
[published releases](https://github.com/lattices-dev/lattices/releases) remain
the reference for what each download includes.

Start with the [Mac app](/#install), or explore the
[workspace API](/lattices/docs/api) and decide what you want to put to work.

# Scout backgrounds

An extension of Scout Web’s Lit Control Room identity. Neutral graphite, silver ink, and very sparse signal lime. These assets are decorative atmosphere, never live status.

Audience: operators looking at Scout for long sessions. This study is a comparison viewer; production screens should use lower intensity and preserve uninterrupted text areas.

Four original illustrations: Listening station, Relay islands, Signal grove, Scout at rest. Four live procedural backgrounds: ASCII terrain, Dither tide, Contour drift, Halftone orbit. The illustration set can also receive ASCII, ordered-dither, and engraved-line treatments. The mascot references the existing Scout dog study.

The Further afield expansion develops the user's preferred relay-island and signal-grove families: Relay archipelago, Hanging observatory, Bridge of whispers, Listening canopy, Resonance grove, and Seed library. Preserve the engraving, graphite materials, negative space, and quiet scale. Add curiosity through coherent sculptural instruments and geological forms. The six new pieces form a three-column gallery above the original collection, collapsing to two columns on mobile. Each artwork has a stable URL fragment for direct preview links.

Keep movement under 2% of the field per second. No flicker, random character refresh, flashing indicators, or animated camera zooms. A static seed maintains continuity. Pause when the document is hidden; honor reduced motion automatically. Canvas resolution and frame rate are bounded. Pause and theme are first-class controls. A left-side falloff protects sample content.

The gallery inherits system sans typography, compact controls, hairlines, graphite dark mode and warm-paper light mode. Its large preview is the artwork; chrome stays secondary. All sample screen content is explicitly marked as a layout sample. It does not represent connected Scout state.

Places between is the third collection: Quiet harbor, Terrace of echoes, Suspended archive, Fiddlehead array, Prism orchard, Rain garden. Keep the established engraving and spatial restraint while varying silhouette: crescent, spiral, horizontal layers, curled stems, branching mobiles, suspended canopy. Show newest collection first without removing earlier work. Original files and URL fragments remain stable.

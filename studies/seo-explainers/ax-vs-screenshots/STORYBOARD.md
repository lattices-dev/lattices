# AX vs screenshots: what UI automation can see

## Video direction
A continuous paper diagram teaches one mechanism. Preserve subject identity; direct attention with sequential reveals and svg-path-draw from the installed svg-line-draw-loader primitive. No narration, no music. Keep copy readable and end on a held useful state.

### Beat 1 (0–9s)
A screenshot is an image: shapes, colors, and visible text. A tool must interpret the pixels to locate a button.

### Beat 2 (9–18s)
AX is structured information supplied by the app. A button may identify its role, title, and supported Press action.

### Beat 3 (18–27s)
Each view has limits. Pixels do not directly encode a button action. AX can omit a custom control or provide incomplete information.

### Beat 4 (27–36s)
Use the evidence the app provides, then verify what changed. Accessibility access and Screen Recording are separate macOS permissions.

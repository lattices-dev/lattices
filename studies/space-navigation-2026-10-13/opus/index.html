Ctrl+←/→ desktop HUD — three directions SIMULATED PREVIEW

Opus study. Each stage is a fake desktop that changes the instant you press — the HUD only acknowledges, it never gates the switch. Beyond the first or last desktop nothing happens, by design. Click a stage (or Tab to it) and use ← / →, or drive all three at once with the buttons.

ready

A · Odometer

The number carries the news; the cells become a ruler.

Idea. At 18 desktops, cells shrink to 7pt and stop being readable. Swap them for a hairline ruler (position at a glance) and let a mechanical two-digit counter roll to the target. Each digit column is one translated strip, so rapid presses just retarget the transform.
  • Tens column is always present once total ≥ 10 (dim leading 0), so 9→10 never reflows.
  • Footprint is fixed per total; the tray never resizes during a burst.
  • Reduce Motion: digits snap, ruler snaps.

B · Lens

The active mark stretches with travel, then settles.

Idea. One bright pill whose two edges animate at different speeds: the leading edge snaps (70ms) to the target, the trailing edge follows (240ms). A single press reads as a quick lunge; a held sweep reads as a long streak from where you started, and contracts the moment you stop. Reversal swaps which edge leads, so the streak folds back on itself without a special case.
  • Neighbours of the lens grow slightly — a Dock-like lens without magnifying anything.
  • Two animatable numbers (left, right). No trail buffer, no blur.
  • Reduce Motion: pill jumps; no stretch.

C · Wake + origin

Keep today's cells; remember where the burst began.

Idea. Closest to the shipped bezel. Every cell you pass flashes to full and decays over 700ms, so a sweep leaves a comet tail and the tail's length is your speed. A small cyan dot under the cell where the burst started answers "how do I get back?" — it is cleared when the tray dismisses.
  • State: one origin index + per-cell opacity the compositor fades. Nothing retained after dismiss.
  • Reversal re-lights cells you return through; the origin dot stays put.
  • Reduce Motion: no decay — only the active cell and origin dot.

Recommendation

Ship C, borrow A's numeral. C keeps the Lattices cell language and adds the one piece of information the current HUD lacks during a burst — where you came from — at the cost of one integer. Replace the "DESKTOP N" label with A's fixed-width rolling two-digit counter so 9→10 and fast sweeps never reflow. Hold B as the direction for > 12 desktops if 7pt cells prove illegible; its stretch is the most expressive answer to speed, but it departs furthest from the logo-cell identity.

Native mapping. A: two Text columns in a clipped frame with .offset(y:), or .contentTransition(.numericText()). B: a Capsule positioned by two @State edges with separate .animation durations. C: per-cell opacity driven by a timestamp array in SpaceBezelState (18 Doubles) plus originIndex; set on show, cleared in dismiss(). All three keep the existing panel, timer and token logic, and none touches the switch path.

Limitations. Browser approximation: CSS easing stands in for SwiftUI springs; tray size and fonts are eyeballed from SpaceSwitchBezel.swift, not measured; travelEdge/bottom positions and multi-display are not simulated; not tested with real key-repeat timing from SpaceSwitchInterceptor.

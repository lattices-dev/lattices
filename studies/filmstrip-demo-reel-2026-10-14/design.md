# Room to move
The film feels like an architectural tour: the app supplies movement, and the titles supply orientation.

- Canvas: 3440×1440, native ultrawide aspect; no crop of source capture.
- Palette: graphite #22211e, ivory #ede9df, secondary #b9b6a8; app footage retains original colors.
- Type: locally bundled Inter, 400/500. Title 220px; supporting topic 66px; captions 48px; keyboard hints 30px.
- Focal element: native filmstrip in the middle; keep annotations out of its way.
- Edge anchors: lower-left chapter label with a quiet rule; lower-right keyboard hint.
- Supporting detail: chapter index 01–03. Background: existing architectural L plate for open/close.
- Corners: square typography layouts; no added rounded card chrome.
- Spacing: 120–232px edge margins; 24–40px between labels.
- Depth: flat typography. No fake camera pushes, sped-up transitions, invented data or decorative app effects.
